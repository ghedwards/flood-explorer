A propagation prototype

Synthetic ocean depths and a prescribed uplift/subsidence pulse. This is not an earthquake scenario or a prediction for a real coast. It does not model wave breaking, shoreline run-up or inundation.

Equations and next steps

The Rust solver advances the linear shallow-water equations using either a staggered-grid method or a finite-volume method. Gravity and ocean depth determine wave speed; a numerical sponge damps waves at open edges. Land is fixed and reflecting.

Next: finer coastal and river-bed terrain with consistent vertical datums, earthquake deformation sources, nonlinear wetting/drying, and tsunami benchmark validation. Rivers and lakes need their own initial water levels and flow boundaries; they are not simulated yet.
