Equations and next steps

The regional methods solve linear shallow-water equations with reflecting land. The dispersive comparison adds wavelength-dependent speeds in a uniform ocean. Separate nonlinear local tests allow water to flood and drain over land; all methods use approximate outer boundaries.

Next: verified coastal and river-bed terrain with consistent vertical datums, earthquake deformation sources, offshore-to-estuary coupling, and tsunami benchmark validation. The separate nonlinear estuary tests include wetting/drying and friction. They have closed outer walls and an optional sponge, with no prescribed river inflow or offshore tsunami boundary. Real local terrain still needs independent bathymetry and vertical-datum verification.
